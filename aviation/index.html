John Belz's Aviation Page





On 9/9/9 I passed my checkride and became a certifiable pilot. I enjoy backcountry flying and recreation, in my adopted state of Utah and elsewhere.



The Utah Back Country Pilots (UBCP) Association hosts an annual fly-in at the Ibex dry lakebed in southwest Utah. A great opportunity to soak in some unique scenery, chat with other pilots and have a tasty lunch.



A visit to Death Valley, California, March 2026. Includes a stop at Furnace Creek Airport (211 feet below sea level), Chicken Strip and Saline Valley Warm Springs, and Owens Lake/Lone Pine at the base of Mount Whitney.



The 185 is a great platform from which to view the fall colors in the Wasatch Range. Big and Little Cottonwood Canyons, Mount Timpanogos and Morgan County were resplendent.
