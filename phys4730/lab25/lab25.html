Exercise 2.

In lecture, the instructor introduced the notebook neuronz.ipynb. Conduct your own experiments with this notebook, to see if you can get a feel for the interplay between the hidden neurons' activation functions and the desired output. Some things to try:

  • There's nothing magical about the tanh function; the activation function just has to be nonlinear. Can you find other activation functions that produce a better representation of sin(x)?

  • Or, work it the other way. Can you find training functions other than sin(x) that this network will better reproduce?

  • BONUS CHALLENGE: Figure out the indexing changes needed to add a third neuron to the hidden layer. Does this improve your output at all?
