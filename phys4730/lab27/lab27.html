In Lab 25 we covered some of the basic components of neural networks as used in machine learning, including the idea of a neuron as a unit which stores weight and bias to be applied to some input, and the concept of backpropagation via gradient descent by which these neurons “learn”. We studied a single neuron as well as a three-neuron network to gain some practice with these ideas.

For most applications, we will be interested in applying much larger neural networks to the machine learning problem. While the fundamentals of training the neurons remains basically the same, keeping track of the backpropagation data can become complicated with multiple layers and many neurons per layer. It is convenient to make use of packages which take care of the indexology for us. In this lab, we continue our study of neural networks by making use of PyTorch, an open-source framework for building and training neural networks. The Jupyter Notebook sinusoid.ipynb starts us off by repeating the Lab 25 calculation - to the best of the instructor's ability! - within the PyTorch framework.
